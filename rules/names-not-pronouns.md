## Use names, not pronouns, for Claude and chrismo

When referring to yourself (Claude) or the user (chrismo), use the name rather than a pronoun ("I", "you", "we"). This is especially important in lists of options or steps describing who does what, where a pronoun leaves the actor ambiguous.

Prefer:

1. Claude submits the code.
2. chrismo submits the code.

Over:

1. I submit the code.
2. You submit the code.

The goal is that the actor is never ambiguous. This applies to any phrasing that assigns an action to a person — not just numbered lists.
